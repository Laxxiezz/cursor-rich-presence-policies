Privacy Policy

Application: Cursor Rich Presence

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

Cursor Rich Presence is a Discord application intended to display custom activity information on Discord profiles.

2. Information and Activity Display

Activity details, state text, images, and other information configured by users may be displayed to other Discord users according to Discord's activity and privacy settings. Users should not enter sensitive or confidential information into activity fields.

3. Data Collection by the Application Owner

The application owner does not operate a dedicated server or database for this application to intentionally collect users' personal information. The application uses local software and Discord's services to display activity. Third-party software may have its own data handling practices.

4. Discord and Third-Party Services

Discord may process account and activity information under its own Privacy Policy. Third-party software used with this application is subject to its own privacy practices.

5. Sharing and Sale of Information

The application owner does not sell personal information. Information displayed through Discord activity may be visible to others depending on the user's Discord settings.

6. Security

Users should keep their Discord account secure and avoid displaying sensitive information in their activity.

7. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application owner through Discord.
